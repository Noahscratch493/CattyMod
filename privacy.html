Privacy Policy

Hey there! Here's the deal with CattyMod and your info:

We use Lovable to see how many people use CattyMod. This helps the site work better. You can't turn this off, but we only track basic usage—nothing personal.

If you use cloud variables, TurboWarp's servers may temporarily log your username and project ID for up to 14 days. These variables are public while people are connected, so anything you put in them can be seen by others. Other cloud servers aren’t managed by us, so we can’t control what happens there.

CattyMod has a community feature for sharing projects, but it’s totally optional. We also recommend not putting any personal info anywhere on CattyMod, just to be safe.

Good news: no ads! 🎉

Projects from Scratch follow Scratch's Privacy Policy. Projects from the Explore Page follow Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Thanks for using CattyMod! 💖

Introduction: This Privacy Policy explains how CattyMod collects, uses, and shares information. By using CattyMod, you agree to the practices described below.

Information Collection: CattyMod tracks how many users use this site through Lovable. This information is required for functionality and cannot be disabled.

Cloud Variables: When you use cloud variables, TurboWarp's Cloud Servers may log your username and project ID for up to 14 days. These values are publicly accessible to anyone connected simultaneously. Data on other cloud servers is outside our control.

Community Features: CattyMod provides optional community features that allow you to share projects. Participation is voluntary. Avoid posting personal information in projects or variables.

Advertisements: CattyMod does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Policies: Projects sourced from Scratch are governed by Scratch's Privacy Policy. Projects sourced from the CattyMod Explore Page are governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Data Security: While CattyMod strives to maintain secure systems, the platform cannot guarantee the security of all information shared via cloud variables or community features.

Changes to This Policy: CattyMod may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page.
